Privacy Policy for Dream Outcome

For the Dream Signs iOS app.

Effective 12 September 2026

Dream Outcome is a dream journal. You write down a dream, later you write what you think it meant, and later still you write what actually happened. This policy explains what we hold, why, and what you can do about it.

It is written to be read. Where something is genuinely a choice you are making, we say so rather than burying it.

The two things worth knowing first

Dreams you post are public by default. Every dream has a visibility setting: Sky means it appears in the app's public feed, visible to anyone using Dream Outcome, and Vault means only you can see it. New dreams are set to Sky. If you want a dream kept private, set it to Vault — you can change it at any time, and moving a dream to Vault removes it from the feed.

Voice recordings never leave your device. If you record a dream by speaking, the audio is stored only in the app's own folder on your iPhone and is transcribed on the device by Apple's speech framework. We never receive the audio. Recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days, because their purpose is to let you check what you mumbled at 3am, not to be an archive.

What we collect

Your dreams and what you write about them. The dream itself, your interpretation, the outcome you recorded, plus the mood, symbols, dates and the "resonance" rating you choose. This is the substance of the app. Dreams set to Sky are shown publicly alongside your handle; dreams set to Vault are not.

An account identity. You can sign in with Apple or with Google.

  • Sign in with Apple can give us a private relay address rather than your real inbox, if you choose to hide your email. We never see your actual address.
  • Continue with Google gives us your real email address and given name.

Either way we store an account identifier and an email address on our authentication server. The app itself keeps only a handle and a display name. On first sign-in you are assigned a generated handle such as dreamer4f9c21a8, so nothing about your name is published unless you change it yourself.

You can record dreams without signing in at all. Those stay on your device until you create an account, at which point they are adopted by it.

An advertising identifier, for free users only. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier. iOS asks your permission before any tracking happens, and if you decline, ads still work but are less relevant. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we also ask for consent separately, and you can revisit that choice under "Ad privacy choices" in the app's settings. Plus subscribers see no ads and no advertising identifier is used.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, your photos, your health data, or your browsing on other apps and websites.

Payments

Plus costs $4.99 per month and is billed by Apple through your App Store account. Payment happens entirely inside Apple's system: we never see your card number, and we receive only whether your subscription is currently active.

Where your data lives

Dreams are stored on your device first — the app works fully offline, which is the point, because dream recall fades within a minute of waking and often somewhere with no signal. When you have a connection, dreams sync to our backend, hosted on Supabase.

If you use the app from outside the region where that backend is hosted, your data is transferred there. For users in the EEA and the UK, that transfer relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Who else is involved

  • Apple — sign-in, subscription billing, on-device speech recognition.
  • Google — sign-in if you use it, and AdMob for advertising to free users.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication.

Each has its own privacy policy governing what it does with what it receives. We do not sell your data, and we do not share your dreams with advertisers.

Using dreams to train AI

We do not use your dreams to train machine-learning models. If that changes, we will ask for your explicit permission first, separately from this policy, and Vault dreams will be excluded regardless of what you agree to. Permission would be something you opt into, not something assumed from continued use of the app, and you would be able to withdraw it.

Moderation

You can report a dream and you can block a dreamer. Reported dreams may be hidden from the feed while we review them, which does not delete them, so a mistake can be undone. Blocking is enforced on our server as well as in the app, so a blocked dreamer cannot reach you by reinstalling.

The app also refuses to post content matching a filter of the worst material at the moment you try to post it.

Deleting your account

There is a delete option in the app, under your profile. It removes your profile, your dreams and your interpretations from our servers, and wipes the local copy on your device. The local wipe happens even if the server call fails, so you are never left with data on the phone you asked to have cleared.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before they age out.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, or object to a particular use. Email hrehman200@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, our lawful bases are: performing our contract with you, for running the account and syncing your dreams; consent, for personalised advertising; and legitimate interests, for moderation and security. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Dream Outcome is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating this page. The effective date above always reflects the current version.
